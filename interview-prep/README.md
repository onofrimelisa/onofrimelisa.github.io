# Interview Preparation

Personal interview prep materials for Senior Software Engineer positions. All documents are living documents — update them as new experiences, insights, or interview learnings come up.

## Documents

### [01 - Storytelling](01-storytelling.md)
**Goal:** Have a polished, natural personal narrative ready for any interview. Covers elevator pitch, career arc, and the "why" behind each career move. Should feel conversational, not rehearsed.

### [02 - HR & Behavioral Questions](02-hr-questions.md)
**Goal:** Prepared answers for classic HR/behavioral questions using the STAR method. Each answer tied to a real experience. Includes tips from Silver.dev on American work culture, connecting with the interviewer, and achieving "Strong Yes" evaluations.

### [03 - Technical Questions](03-technical-questions.md)
**Goal:** Prepared answers for common technical interview questions (system design, architecture decisions, debugging, etc.). Based on real interview experiences and challenges I've participated in.

### [04 - Key Concepts](04-key-concepts.md)
**Goal:** Quick-reference cheat sheet of core concepts a Senior Software Engineer must know. Read before interviews to refresh vocabulary and be able to explain each concept clearly. Covers: Java/Spring, microservices patterns, design principles, databases, cloud, observability, and more.

### [05 - Experience Cases](05-experience-cases.md)
**Goal:** Concrete, detailed examples from my career that demonstrate technical depth, leadership, and problem-solving. Ready to use when an interviewer says "tell me about a time when..." or "walk me through your architecture."

## How to Use

1. **Before an interview:** Re-read 01 (storytelling) and 04 (key concepts) as warm-up
2. **For behavioral rounds:** Review 02 (HR questions) and 05 (experience cases)
3. **For technical rounds:** Review 03 (technical questions) and 04 (key concepts)
4. **After an interview:** Update documents with new questions you were asked, things you wish you'd said better, and concepts you need to study
