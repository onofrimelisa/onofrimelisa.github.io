## Storytelling Tips (from Silver.dev)

- **Use "I" not "we"** — Show personal agency. You can acknowledge the team but make your contribution clear.
- **STAR method** — Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep stories structured.
- **No victim narratives** — Don't frame things as happening *to* you. Frame them as challenges you *chose* to tackle.
- **Don't seek approval** — Don't ask "does that make sense?" after every answer. Be confident.
- **Sell authentically** — Short-term: optimize for offers. Long-term: find genuine fit. Both matter.
