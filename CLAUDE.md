## Project Overview

This repo holds my professional profile: resume, portfolio site, and interview preparation materials.

## Interview Prep Convention

When creating or updating an interview prep file for a specific company (`interview-prep/<company>.md`), always include an **Adapted Pitch** section with storytelling customized for that company and job description. The pitch must:

- Highlight the specific experiences from my career that match the JD requirements
- Use vocabulary and framing relevant to the company's domain and product
- Connect the thread of my career to what the company does
- NOT be a generic pitch reused across companies — each one must be tailored

The general storytelling templates in `interview-prep/01-storytelling.md` are the base; each company file adapts and extends them for that specific opportunity.
