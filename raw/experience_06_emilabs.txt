EXPERIENCE #6 - EMI LABS
================================================================================

Role: Semi-Senior Software Engineer
Company: Emi Labs (AI-powered workforce hiring platform)
Period: August 2023 - April 2024 (9 months)
Type: Full-time

About the company:
  Emi Labs is an AI-driven platform that automates high-volume recruitment
  processes (attracting, screening, interviewing, onboarding) for major
  enterprises like Walmart, OXXO, Coca-Cola FEMSA, and Heineken. The
  platform processes 12M+ conversations via WhatsApp, Messenger, and SMS
  across 50+ enterprise clients in Mexico and the US.

Description:
  Part of the integrations team, responsible for building and maintaining
  the integration layer that enabled clients in Mexico and the US to connect
  to Emi's ecosystem. Balanced two parallel objectives: customizing the
  product to meet specific client requirements while continuing to develop
  a generic, scalable, cross-client solution.

Tech stack & tools:
  - Backend: TypeScript, Java, Go
  - Databases: SQL and NoSQL
  - Cloud: AWS

Key highlights:
  - Built client-facing integrations for the US and Mexico markets
  - Designed scalable, cross-client architecture while delivering
    client-specific customizations
  - AI/ML product domain — conversational AI platform
  - Enterprise clients: Walmart, OXXO, Coca-Cola FEMSA, Heineken
  - Balancing product customization with platform scalability
