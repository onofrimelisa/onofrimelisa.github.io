================================================================================
EXPERIENCE #4 - MERCADO LIBRE
================================================================================

Company: Mercado Libre (MELI - NASDAQ listed, largest e-commerce and
  fintech company in Latin America)
Department: Platform Security 360 - Authorizations
Period: February 2021 - July 2022 (1 year 6 months)
Type: Full-time

Roles:
  1) Semi-Senior Software Engineer (March 2022 - July 2022) - Promoted
  2) Software Development Analyst (February 2021 - March 2022)

Description:
  Part of the team responsible for revolutionizing the user authorization
  system across all of Mercado Libre, designing a new authorization model
  inspired by AWS IAM with policy-based access control. Internal clients
  were other Mercado Libre engineering teams who integrated their services
  with the authorization platform.

  Two integration patterns were developed:
  - SDKs in multiple languages (Java, Go, Python, Node.js) for direct
    integration
  - Sidecar deployment model using Docker, where the authorization service
    ran alongside the consuming application in the same instance

  Services had to be extremely performant, efficient, and error-free since
  they were in the critical path of virtually every service across
  Mercado Libre, impacting millions of users.

Tech stack & tools:
  - Backend: Java, Go
  - SDKs: Java, Go, Python, Node.js
  - Frontend: React
  - Architecture: Microservices
  - Databases: SQL and NoSQL
  - Monitoring: Datadog, Kibana, NewRelic
  - CI/CD: Jenkins, Gitflow
  - Testing: Unit, Integration, and Stress tests
  - Methodology: Scrum, JIRA
  - Infrastructure: Docker (sidecar pattern)

Key highlights:
  - Promoted from Analyst to Semi-Senior Engineer within 1 year
  - Built platform-level infrastructure used by every team at Mercado Libre
  - AWS IAM-inspired policy-based authorization model
  - High-traffic, mission-critical services impacting millions of users
  - Multi-language SDK development (Java, Go, Python, Node.js)
  - Sidecar deployment pattern with Docker
  - On-call rotation for production incident response
  - Stress testing and performance optimization at massive scale
  - Internal clients: other MELI engineering teams
